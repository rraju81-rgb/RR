Fitment

  1. Open the bar end: remove the OEM bar-end weight, or trim a closed grip end.
  2. Measure the bar's inside diameter and pick the S, M or L expander sleeve. If the bar has a threaded OEM insert, use the flat adapter and a bolt into that thread instead.
  3. Push the adapter in until it seats on the bar end, set the arm on the serration at the lever's angle, and fit the M6 bolt finger tight.
  4. Slide the blade in its slot for at least 10 mm all round the lever, then torque the M5 screws to 4 N·m and the M6 bolt to 8 N·m.
  5. Pull the lever through its full travel and turn the bars lock to lock: nothing may touch.
