The joint

THREE PIECES.
FOUR BOW-TIES.

A 1.3 m spoiler is too big for one print, so we print it in three sections. Every seam is locked by two bow-tie (double-dovetail) keys set into the hidden underside, with structural adhesive across the whole joint face.

The keys sit flush with the underside, under the mounting tape. On top, the seams are filled, primed and painted, so the finished spoiler shows one clean surface.
