How it holds

Steel on the four faces that don't light up

The stock head sits on a flexible rubber stalk. When the stalk tears or its mount cracks, the head droops or hangs on its wires. The cap takes the stalk out of the load path: the head is held by the cap, and the cap by a screw.

  • Grips four faces, leaves the lens open.Top, bottom, back and tip are steel. The lit face of the lens is never covered, so the indicator still shows across its full width.
  • Foam does the gripping.Self-adhesive EPDM: 1 mm on the plates (squeezed to 0.8), 2 mm on the back wall (1.5), 3 mm at the tip (2.0). It is squeezed by a fifth to a third, which takes up the lamp's size spread and vibration and keeps steel off the plastic.
  • Tabs stop it sliding off.Four keeper tabs hook 2.05 mm over the sloped strip above and below the lens. They touch only that strip, never the lit face.
  • One screw takes the load.The ear sits on the boss beside the socket and reuses its screw (M5 assumed). The slot allows ±2 mm to line it up.
  • Same part on both sides.The cap is symmetric top to bottom, so you turn it over for the other side. Front and rear use the same lamp, so the channel is identical; only the ear changes if the screws sit in different places.

Make it

Card first, then steel

A card copy costs nothing and shows a wrong measurement before the laser does.

  1. Update cad/params.py with your measurements and run python3 build.py. Every check must pass.
  2. Print out/template_1to1.pdf at 100 %. Check the 100 mm bar with a ruler, glue it to 1 mm card, cut, fold and try it on the lamp.
  3. Send out/cap_blank.dxf to a laser cutter: layer CUT only, 1.5 mm stainless 304, deburred.
  4. Bend B1 to B8 in order. B7 and B8 form a channel 21.6 mm inside: use a gooseneck punch, or bend over a 21.6 mm form block in a vice.
  5. Finish: bead blast and black textured powder coat to match the lamp body, or leave it brushed. The liner gaps absorb the coating.

Fit it

Ten minutes with a hex key

  1. If the head has torn off its stalk, repair the wiring first (solder and heat-shrink, or a new connector) and check that it flashes.
  2. Clean the inside of the cap and stick the foam strips to the cap, not to the lamp: 1 mm on the plates and tabs, 2 mm on the back wall, 3 mm on the tip wall.
  3. Hold the head in its normal position. Slide the cap over it from the tip until the ear meets the boss. The tabs drop over the sloped edge of the lens.
  4. Fit the screw and washer through the ear slot into the boss. Snug it, about 2 N·m for a screw into plastic. Don't use threadlocker in plastic; it can crack the boss.
  5. Push the lamp tip in every direction: it should not move. Check from in front of and behind the bike that the whole lens lights.

References

Where the idea comes from

Bikers Billet sell front and rear brackets for the 2025 390 Adventure that do the same job: a folded steel cover over the lamp, held by one screw. A rider's install reel shows the black rear version blending in with the lamp. This design keeps that idea and changes the details: a foam liner instead of steel on plastic, tabs that touch only the lens edge, a slotted ear with adjustment, and one symmetric part for both sides.

Independent design. Not a KTM part; not affiliated with KTM or Bikers Billet.
