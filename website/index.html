One print vs Many prints

A standard 220 mm printer must slice a large model into 12–16 pieces, then glue, sand, fill and repaint it, adding ₹2,000–4,000 per project and days of labour, with every glue joint a weak point and a visible seam. Our Kobra 3 Max prints the same model on its 420 × 420 × 500 mm (88-litre) bed as one seamless piece: stronger, cleaner, faster, and cheaper at XL sizes because assembly labour disappears entirely.
