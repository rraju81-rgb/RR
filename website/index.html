FAQ

Common questions

What file formats do you accept?

STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP are ideal. If you only have a drawing, photo or physical object, we can model it for you (design fee applies).

How long does printing take?

Most single parts are ready in 24–72 hours. Larger parts and batches take longer; your quote includes a firm timeline. Rush jobs are available on request.

How accurate are the prints?

Typical FDM tolerance is ±0.2 mm; resin is around ±0.05 mm. For press-fits and threads, tell us the critical dimensions and we'll tune for them.

Is there a minimum order?

No. Single parts are welcome. Batches of 10+ get volume pricing.

Do you ship?

Yes. We ship nationwide with tracking, or you can pick up locally for free.

Is my design kept confidential?

Always. Your files are used only for your order and never shared. We're happy to sign an NDA.
