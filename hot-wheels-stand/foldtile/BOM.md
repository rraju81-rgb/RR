# Flat-fold hook tile rack: parts

| Item | 3-car | 5-car |
|---|---|---|
| Printed tile (`stl/hook_tile_print.stl`, already in print orientation) | 3 | 5 |
| Wall screw (4 mm wood screw with countersunk head, plus plug if needed) | 6 | 10 |

Rack length 336 mm (3-car) / 560 mm (5-car); height 24 mm; thickness 8 mm. Each tile is 26.4 g.

**Print:** PETG or PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20% infill, **no supports**, tile front face down (the STL already is). Slow the first layers. Each tile comes off the bed with the arm already hinged in place.

**Fit:** if an arm is stiff, swing it back and forth a few times.

**Mount:** draw a level line. Push tiles together (the tongue enters the groove) and screw them to the wall through the two countersunk holes in each tile. Swing each arm out, hang one card per arm through the hang hole.
Rows: repeat with a second row 185 mm lower (cards are 165 mm tall).
