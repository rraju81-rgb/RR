# 4-car display kit
| Part | File | Qty | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall strip | wall_strip.stl | 1 | rear face down, no supports |
| Hinge clip, depth j = 0..3 | hinge_clip_j0..j3.stl | 1 each | standing, brim, no supports |
| Lock peg | lock_peg.stl | 4 | head down |
| Ledge | ledge.stl | 4 | standing |
Plates: plate_clips.stl, plate_ledges.stl, plate_pegs.stl (256 mm bed).
Mount: screw the strip(s) to the wall (second strip: press it straight onto the first strip's dovetail from the front first). Slide each clip onto the strip from the top; clip j with its foot
at y = 30 + 60 j mm (its lock hole then lines up with a peg hole in the strip). Push a peg through the clip's hole into the strip hole. Drop each ledge on its pin. The closed ledge clicks into a dimple (lift the ledge about 1 mm to open it).
Stack height 238 mm.
