# 14 cars on two 40 cm strips (7 + 7)
| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| wall_strip_400.stl (or part1 + part2) | 2 |
| hinge_clip_j0 ... hinge_clip_j6 | 2 of each (one set per strip) |
| ledge.stl | 14 |
Mount the second strip directly above the first, same x, with its bottom end 491 mm above the bottom end of the first
(that leaves 91 mm of bare wall between them). On each strip the clips go j0 at the bottom up to j6 at the top.
Why not 0-1-2-3-4-5-6-5-4-3-2-1-0: when the depth goes back down, a card sits in front of the rack above it, so the blister
of the upper card hits it and every car above is hidden behind it. Starting again at depth 0 needs the 91 mm gap
so the last card of the lower strip ends before the first card of the upper strip begins.
