# 4-car display kit (hook strip + solid hinge clips)
| Part | File | Qty | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall strip with hook lips | wall_strip.stl | 1 | standing on its long edge (as exported), brim, no supports |
| Hinge clip, depth j = 0..3 | hinge_clip_j0..j3.stl | 1 each | standing (as exported), no supports (finger bridges 31 mm between the cheeks) |
| Ledge | ledge.stl | 4 | standing (as exported) |
Mount: screw the strip(s) to the wall (second strip: press it straight onto the first strip's dovetail from the front first).
Hang each clip: hold it 6 mm above its hook lip (clip j uses lip 1, 4, 7, ... counted from the bottom, starting at 0), push it against the strip
with the two cheeks either side of the strip, and let it drop - the finger falls behind the lip. To remove: lift 6 mm and pull toward you.
Drop each ledge on its pin. It rests on the support arm; the nub under it stops it at 0 degrees and the bump holds it closed
(lift the ledge about 1 mm to swing it open). Stack height 239 mm.
