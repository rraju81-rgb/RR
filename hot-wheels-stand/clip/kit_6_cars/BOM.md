# 6-car display kit
| Part | File | Qty | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall strip with stopper (bottom strip) | wall_strip_base.stl | 1 | rear face down, 3 x 5 mm screw holes |
| Upper wall strip (dovetail pocket, joins the base strip) | wall_strip_top.stl | 1 | front face down |

| Hinge clip, depth j = 0..5 | hinge_clip_j0..j5.stl | 1 each | standing, brim, all 30 mm tall |
| Ledge | ledge_x6.stl | 6 | standing |
Plates: plate_clips.stl (all clips) and plate_ledges.stl (6 ledges) are laid out for a 256 mm bed.
Mount: screw the strip to the wall, slide clip j onto the strip from the top, rest the lowest on the stopper, space the others 60 mm apart (nub in a dimple),
drop each ledge on the pin. Clip j0 is the lowest rack. The total height of the stack is 350 mm (two strips: push the upper strip onto the base strip tongue from the front, dovetail locks it).
