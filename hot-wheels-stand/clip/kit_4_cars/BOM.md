# 4-car display kit
| Part | File | Qty | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall strip with stopper (bottom strip) | wall_strip_base.stl | 1 | rear face down, 3 x 5 mm screw holes |
| Hinge clip, depth j = 0..3 | hinge_clip_j0..j3.stl | 1 each | standing, brim, all 30 mm tall |
| Ledge | ledge_x4.stl | 4 | standing |
Plates: plate_clips.stl (all clips) and plate_ledges.stl (4 ledges) are laid out for a 256 mm bed.
Mount: screw the strip to the wall, slide clip j onto the strip from the top, rest the lowest on the stopper, space the others 60 mm apart (nub in a dimple),
drop each ledge on the pin. Clip j0 is the lowest rack. The total height of the stack is 230 mm and fits one 240 mm strip.
