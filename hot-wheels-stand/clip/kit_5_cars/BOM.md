# 5-car display kit
| Part | File | Qty | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall strip (25 mm dovetail rail, 5 mm screw holes, 6 mm ratchet teeth) | wall_strip.stl | 2 | rear face down (flat, teeth up) |
| Hinge clip with ratchet pawl + release tab, depth j = 0..4 | hinge_clip_j0..j4.stl | 1 each | standing, brim, all 38 mm tall |
| Ledge | ledge_x5.stl | 5 | standing |
Plates: plate_clips.stl (all clips) and plate_ledges.stl (5 ledges) are laid out for a 256 mm bed.
Mount: screw the strip to the wall, slide each clip onto the strip from the BOTTOM end and push it up: it clicks one 6 mm step at a time and cannot slide back down. Foot of clip j at y = 20 + 60 j mm. To lower a clip, pull its release tab (top, front) forward and slide it down,
drop each ledge on the pin. Clip j0 is the lowest rack. The total height of the stack is 290 mm (two identical strips: screw the first strip up, then press the second strip onto its Z-lock from the front).
