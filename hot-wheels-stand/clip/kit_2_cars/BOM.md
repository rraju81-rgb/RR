# 2-car display kit
| Part | File | Qty | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall strip (25 mm dovetail rail, 5 mm screw holes, stop holes every 20 mm) | wall_strip.stl | 1 | front face down |
| Hinge clip, depth j = 0..1 | hinge_clip_j0..j1.stl | 1 each | standing, brim, all 30 mm tall |
| Ledge | ledge_x2.stl | 2 | standing |
Plates: plate_clips.stl (all clips) and plate_ledges.stl (2 ledges) are laid out for a 256 mm bed.
Mount: screw the strip to the wall, slide clip j onto the strip from the top, put the lowest clip with its foot 20 mm above the strip bottom and the others 60 mm apart, so the spring nub of each clip snaps into a stop hole,
drop each ledge on the pin. Clip j0 is the lowest rack. The total height of the stack is 110 mm (fits one 240 mm strip).
