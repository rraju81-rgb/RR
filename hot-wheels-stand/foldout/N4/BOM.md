# 4-stall fold-down rack: bill of materials and settings
Printed (PETG): see the STL files. The arms are exported flat (plate on the bed). The panel and the
wall posts are longer than most print beds; print them in sections, or cut the bars from 6 mm plywood or acrylic.
Overall: panel length 375 mm, depth behind the panel D = 39.0 mm, open reach about 375 mm.

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Joint bolts M3 x 14 + M3 nut | 16 | arm joints (nut sits in the pocket in the arm) |
| Hinge bolts M3 x 20 + lock nut + 2 spring washers | 4 | hinge H and B2 pivot F; tighten the H pair for hinge friction |
| Spring-post bolts M3 x 22 | 2 | on the panel bar at height 64 mm |
| Extension springs | 2 | rate 0.0233 N/mm each, free length about 60 mm, initial tension about 1.40 N each (initial tension = rate x free length) |
| Magnets 6 x 2 mm | 4 | two on the panel top bar, two on the wall crossbar (pockets 6.2 mm) |
| Wall screws M4 + plugs | 4 | in the post holes at heights 205 mm and 345 mm |
| Spacer tubes (printed) | 4 | spacer_x4.stl |

Spring tensions: 2.8 N when closed rising to 4.6 N when open (each).
Wall anchor height 185 mm. Hinge friction needed to stay put with a spring error of +-10%: 55.2 N mm.
