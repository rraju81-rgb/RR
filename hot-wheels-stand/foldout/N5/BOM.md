# 5-stall fold-down rack: bill of materials and settings
Printed (PETG): see the STL files. The arms are exported flat (plate on the bed). The panel and the
wall posts are longer than most print beds; print them in sections, or cut the bars from 6 mm plywood or acrylic.
Overall: panel length 421 mm, depth behind the panel D = 45.5 mm, open reach about 421 mm.

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Joint bolts M3 x 14 + M3 nut | 20 | arm joints (nut sits in the pocket in the arm) |
| Hinge bolts M3 x 20 + lock nut + 2 spring washers | 4 | hinge H and B2 pivot F; tighten the H pair for hinge friction |
| Spring-post bolts M3 x 22 | 2 | on the panel bar at height 64 mm |
| Extension springs | 2 | rate 0.0283 N/mm each, free length about 60 mm, initial tension about 1.70 N each (initial tension = rate x free length) |
| Magnets 6 x 2 mm | 4 | two on the panel top bar, two on the wall crossbar (pockets 6.2 mm) |
| Wall screws M4 + plugs | 4 | in the post holes at heights 230 mm and 391 mm |
| Spacer tubes (printed) | 4 | spacer_x4.stl |

Spring tensions: 4.1 N when closed rising to 6.2 N when open (each).
Wall anchor height 210 mm. Hinge friction needed to stay put with a spring error of +-10%: 75.9 N mm.
