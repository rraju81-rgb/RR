# Accordion Hook Rack v4: QA Report (3-car and 5-car)

Generated by `rack_test.py` (re-run: `python3 rack_test.py 3 5`). Raw results: `qa_3.json`, `qa_5.json`.
All checks are **computational (CAD) tests**. No physical prototype has been printed or loaded yet; see "What is NOT verified".

## Design in one paragraph
A horizontal printed rail is screwed to the wall. A lazy-tongs (scissor) frame of identical bars is bolted to it with M4 hardware. Each card hangs from a printed hook on the end of its own bar. The bottom row rides in the rail slot, so the hooks stay level at every opening angle (this removes the cantilever droop that grew with N in the earlier wall-plate design). Pull the end hook right to open, push left to fold. **Cards must be removed before folding** (cards are 105 mm wide; hooks are 112 mm apart open and 51 mm apart folded).

Printed designs: **3** (`link_A`, `link_B`, `rail segment`). No snaps, no print-in-place, no springs.

| printed parts | 3 link A, 3 link B, 2 rail segments | 5 link A, 5 link B, 3 rail segments |

## What is NOT verified
- **No physical print yet.** Print one `link_A` + `link_B` + a rail segment and test-fit the bolts before selling (hole size depends on the printer).
- The **hang-hole position/size (7 mm, 12 mm from the card top) is an assumption** about the card. Measure real cards; the hook peg is 4.8 mm.
- Card weight 40 g and PETG allowables are assumed values.
- Droop and structural checks are analytic bounds, not a finite-element analysis.
- Long-term creep of PETG under load, UV, and cycle life are untested.

## Changes made because of testing
1. Snap-fit printed pins failed the strength/strain checks (bending SF 0.8; 24-93% over the strain limit) and were replaced with M4 bolts and nylock nuts.
2. The earlier slotted wall-plate hung the lattice as a cantilever; the corrected (unclipped) load analysis showed droop up to 26 mm at 5 cars. It was replaced by the rail-guided design.
3. Slot ends were too short (bolt/rail overlap at the stop); extended by one bolt radius plus 0.4 mm.
4. Slot widened 4.8 to 5.0 mm after the tolerance Monte Carlo.
