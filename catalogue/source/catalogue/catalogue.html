GARAGE · how it works

Swing out. Swap. Swing shut.

1 · Swap a card. Swing the door out (it opens to 135°), lift the card out and drop the next one in. The other five never move.
2 · Lift-off ledges. Each ledge sits on a Ø5 mm pin. Lift it 15.5 mm to take it off for cleaning or to swap in a spare.
3 · Make it a Twin. A mirrored left rack hangs 27.5 mm lower, so the shelves alternate and both sides open freely.

PODIUM · how it works

Open it. Load it. Fold it away.

1 · Flip the leg. It swings from flat to a hard stop at 35°. The rack's own weight keeps it shut, so there is nothing to adjust or slip.
2 · Slide the cards in. Each card goes in from the open end and stops at exactly 108 mm. It slides out the same way.
3 · Fold it away. Folded flat it is 31–45 mm thick: ready for a drawer, a shelf, a gift box or the courier.
Made in one piece. It prints in one go, hinge included, with no supports. There is nothing to sand or assemble.

Fit & care

Will my cars fit?

  • Standard 1:64 mainline cardsCards of about 105–108 × 165 mm, with a blister up to 42 mm deep. That covers most mainline cards from the popular brands.
  • Tall cards stand proudThe top card stands above the rack, about 145 mm on PODIUM. Leave that headroom on a shelf.
  • Easy on your wall3 screws and wall plugs come in the box. GRID needs about 11 cm of free wall on its open side for loading.
  • Treat the hinges kindlyDon't force a GARAGE door past 135°. If a pin ever snaps, spare ledges and wall mounts are available.
